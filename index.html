What is Surya Namaskar?

Surya Namaskar (Sun Salutation) is a sequence of 10 powerful yoga poses performed in a smooth flow. It is a complete practice that combines physical exercise, breathing and mind. The Surya Namaskar Yajna (SNY) is a 16-day event dedicated to promoting this healthy lifestyle, fostering community and collective well-being.

Why & How to Participate

Why Participate?

  • Introduce and practice Yoga in our daily lives.
  • Learn simple asanas for physical and intellectual development.
  • Lead a healthy lifestyle with peace, balance, and eternal bliss.
  • Participating children will receive a certificate.
  • Practice Surya Namaskar at home and track the count.
  • Enter the daily count in the mobile app.

Goals & Daily Routine

  • Individual Goal: Perform 1 Surya Namaskar (SN) set and 5 Yogasanas daily.
  • Weekly Target: Aim for 100+ Surya Namaskars per week.
  • Operational Rhythm: Encouraging daily calls at the Shakha and Gat-Pat levels to maintain momentum.

Benefits & Practice Guidelines

Physical Benefits

  • Improves flexibility & muscle strength
  • Enhances posture and balance
  • Boosts digestion and metabolism
  • Strengthens the heart and lungs

Mental Benefits

  • Reduces stress and anxiety
  • Improves focus and concentration
  • Brings calmness and emotional balance

Overall Wellness

  • Energizes the body
  • Helps maintain healthy weight
  • Brings calmness and emotional balance

Best Time to Practice

  • Early morning
  • Empty stomach
  • In a clean airy place

Precautions

  • Practice slowly if you are a beginner
  • Avoid during fever or severe illness
  • Pregnant women should consult a doctor
