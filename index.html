What is Surya Namaskar Yajna?

The Surya Namaskar Yajna (SNY) is a 16-day event from January 14th to February 1st, dedicated to promoting a healthy lifestyle. Participants engage in the practice of Surya Namaskar, or Sun Salutations, a dynamic sequence of 10 yoga postures. This ancient practice is a holistic exercise that invigorates the body, calms the mind, and nurtures the spirit, fostering a sense of community and collective well-being.

Key Benefits of Sun Salutations

Improves Cardiovascular Health

A great cardio workout that helps maintain a healthy heart.

Boosts Immunity

Enhances blood circulation, which helps in strengthening the immune system.

Reduces Stress

Calms the mind, reduces anxiety, and improves concentration.

Enhances Flexibility

Stretches and tones muscles, improving overall body flexibility and posture.
